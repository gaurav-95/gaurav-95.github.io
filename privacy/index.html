Asli India Drive: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Asli India Drive ("the game") is an Android game made by Gourab Hazra ("we"). This policy explains what information the game and the services inside it collect, and why.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information ourselves. The game has no account, no sign-in and no chat. We run no servers that receive your data.

Your progress is stored only on your device: your Kamai balance, cars, upgrades, records and settings. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes this progress.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows full-screen ads between journeys, using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system version and app version
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps

Google collects and uses this data under Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices:

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any personalised ads are shown. You can change that choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings under Google → Ads.
  • A one-time Remove Ads purchase turns ads off permanently.

Purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. We receive only a confirmation of which item was bought, so the game can unlock it and restore it if you reinstall.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
